# Artist / Album / Artwork Research Workflow

Use for an artist, album, song, artwork, exhibition, label, venue, or cultural institution.

## Evidence layers
1. Identification: canonical name, dates, aliases, medium/format, location, identifiers.
2. Primary object: recording, score, artwork, exhibition material, interview, archival document, creator statement.
3. Historical context: institutions, scene, technology, political/economic setting, contemporaneous events.
4. Scholarly context: peer-reviewed research, academic books/chapters, critical editions.
5. Reception: contemporary criticism, interviews, audience/fan discourse, charts/platform data when relevant.
6. Interpretation: connect formal features to historical/social arguments while labeling interpretation.
7. Contradictions: disputed dates, attribution, provenance, authorship, influence, or conflicting accounts.

## Procedure
1. Establish the object and scope.
2. Search primary and institutional sources first where feasible.
3. Search scholarly literature across musicology, art history, cultural studies, and social sciences as appropriate.
4. Build a source dossier and evidence matrix.
5. Verify important biographical, historical, release/exhibition, and attribution claims.
6. Compare creator statements with independent scholarship rather than treating either as automatically decisive.
7. Analyze form, production, circulation, institutions, reception, and social context as relevant.
8. Distinguish documented influence from retrospective interpretation.
9. Draft a paper-style analysis with citations and a clear uncertainty section.

## Output
Produce a dossier with identification, timeline, primary evidence, historical/social context, scholarship, reception, analysis, competing interpretations, contradictions/gaps, and bibliography.

## Guardrails
Promotional biographies, fan wikis, and unsourced databases may help discovery but should not carry major claims alone. Do not infer authorial intent solely from the object. Do not treat popularity as cultural significance without defining the measure.
