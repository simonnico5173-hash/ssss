# Artist / Album / Artwork Research Workflow

Use for an artist, album, song, artwork, exhibition, or creative work.

## Evidence layers

1. Identification: canonical title/name, dates, aliases, medium/format, location, identifiers.
2. Primary evidence: recording, artwork, score, interview, exhibition materials, archival records, creator statements, contemporaneous documents.
3. Context: institutions, scenes, technology, politics, economics, production conditions.
4. Scholarship: peer-reviewed research, academic books/chapters, critical editions, reputable criticism.
5. Reception: contemporary reviews, audience/fan discourse, charts or platform data where relevant.
6. Interpretation: connect formal features to historical/social arguments while clearly labeling interpretation.
7. Contradictions: record disputed dates, authorship, attribution, provenance, or conflicting accounts.

Never treat a promotional biography or fan wiki as equivalent to primary or scholarly evidence.

## Output

Produce a compact dossier with:
- identification
- timeline
- primary evidence
- historical/social context
- scholarship
- reception
- analysis
- competing interpretations
- contradictions and gaps
- bibliography / source list
