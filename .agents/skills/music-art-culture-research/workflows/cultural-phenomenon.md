# Cultural Phenomenon Research Workflow

Use for genres, scenes, fandoms, trends, rituals, subcultures, platform practices, and cultural movements.

## Steps

1. Define the phenomenon and competing definitions.
2. Establish chronology and geography.
3. Identify actors, communities, institutions, infrastructures, technologies, and economic incentives.
4. Examine production, circulation, mediation, participation, and reception.
5. Compare insider/emic accounts with analytical/etic interpretations.
6. Search for counterexamples, missing evidence, and marginalized voices.
7. Compare alternative mechanisms rather than assuming the first plausible explanation.
8. Connect micro practices, meso institutions, and macro structures only where evidence supports the connection.
9. Separate correlation from mechanism and causation.
10. End with the strongest explanation, alternatives, limitations, and evidence gaps.

## Suggested analytical dimensions

- historical context
- geography
- class and labor
- race/ethnicity as historically relevant categories
- gender and sexuality where relevant
- technology and infrastructure
- institutions and gatekeepers
- markets and political economy
- media/platforms
- identity and community
- production and consumption
- circulation and reception
