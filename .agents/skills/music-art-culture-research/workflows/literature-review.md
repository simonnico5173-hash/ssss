# Literature Review Workflow

Use for literature reviews, scoping reviews, systematic reviews, and research-gap mapping.

## Steps

1. Define the research question, scope, period, geography, and inclusion/exclusion criteria.
2. Generate synonyms, historical terminology, related concepts, and competing explanations.
3. Search scholarly sources and bibliographic indexes available to you.
4. Start from strong reviews and landmark works, then trace references backward and citations forward.
5. Screen sources for relevance, scholarly quality, methodological transparency, and direct usefulness.
6. Extract author/year, question, object/sample, method, evidence, findings, limitations, and relevance.
7. Group the literature by themes, methods, schools of thought, chronology, or competing explanations.
8. Identify genuine gaps only after checking terminology and disciplinary differences.
9. Synthesize agreement, disagreement, methodological differences, and unresolved questions.
10. Report search limitations and avoid claiming exhaustiveness unless the protocol supports it.
